Quality check

Text-to-speech occasionally skips, repeats or invents words. The Quality check stage listens to every synthesized clip with Whisper (whisper-large-v3-turbo) and compares what it hears with the script.

Word error rate

The WER is the share of words that differ. Case, punctuation, hyphens ("sub-scored" vs "subscored") and ordinals ("12th" vs "12") are ignored. A clip fails when at least two words are wrong and the errors exceed 15% of its words (the default limit), or when half of it or more is wrong or missing. One word off in a short fragment is within Whisper's own noise and does not count.

Automatic retries

A failing clip is synthesized again with different seeds (up to three times) and the best take is kept. Everything is cached, so running the check again costs nothing unless clips changed.

It also flags clips with a long silence inside (over 1.5 s) or with clipping (distortion).

Noises

The voice sometimes adds sounds of its own (squeaks, breaths, a stray "no" or "bye") before, between or after sentences. Whisper's word timings show where the script is spoken; anything audible outside those words is muted when the clips are fitted. The Log lists the clips that had noises. The original takes in tts/ are not changed, and a New Take keeps its sound until the check runs again. Set mute_noise = false under [qa] in narr.toml to turn this off.

Fixing what's left

The Quality tab lists the clips that still fail, with what the script says and what Whisper heard. For each one:

  • Play Synthesized to listen. Sometimes Whisper is wrong and the clip is fine.
  • New Take for another attempt with a fresh seed.
  • Edit Text… to rephrase it in the Script tab.
  • Accept Anyway when it sounds right.

Acronyms and pronunciation

Voices often stumble on acronyms and version numbers (for example "AIA" or "LEED 5.0"). Create pronunciations.toml next to the video:

"AIA"      = "A I A"
"USGBC"    = "U S G B C"
"LEED 5.0" = "leed five point oh"

These spellings are only used for speech; the script and subtitles keep the real text. Then run Synthesize and Quality check again.
