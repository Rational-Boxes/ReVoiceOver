Reviewing the script

After the Clean script stage the run pauses. The Script tab shows every narration unit: what you said and what will be spoken in its place. Nothing is synthesized until you press Approve & Continue.

The unit list

IconRed: needs attention (dropped words, or a failed quality check). Amber: worth a look. Green: edited by you.
UnitThe unit id, e.g. u0042.
Start, SlotWhere the unit starts in the video, and how long it has before the next one.
NarrationThe text that will be spoken.
LengthCleaned length as a share of what you said.

Moving between units: click a row, or use Up/Down in the list. While editing, Ctrl+Enter accepts the text and moves to the next unit, and Alt+Up/Alt+Down move to the previous/next unit. You can also click a unit on the Timeline. The bar between the list and the editor can be dragged to show more rows.

Use the search box (text or unit id), Flagged only and Changed only to narrow the list. Selecting a unit also selects it on the Timeline, and the other way round.

What the flags mean

dropped real wordsThe model removed a unit that had real content. Often right for garbled speech, sometimes a mistake. Listen and decide.
dropped (filler only)The unit was only "um", "uh" and the like. That part of the video becomes silent.
much shorter than spokenUnder 40% of the original length. Check that nothing important went missing.
longer than spokenOver 110% of the original length. The model may have moved words in from a neighbouring unit, and it may not fit its slot.
synthesized words don't matchThe quality check heard something different.

The editor

The lower half shows the selected unit:

  • As spoken: the transcript, with words the cleanup removed struck out.
  • Narration to synthesize: editable. Words the cleanup added are underlined in green; glossary terms are boxed.
  • Play Original plays what you said; Play Synthesized plays the cloned voice once it exists; New Take re-synthesizes just this unit.
  • clip / slot compares the synthesized length with the time available, and warns when the text changed after synthesis.
  • Use Raw Text and Revert to LLM Text undo the cleanup or your edits.
  • Keep my original voice marks the unit to use your real recording instead of the clone (patch mode).

Edits are committed when you move to another unit or press Ctrl+Enter. Ctrl+Z undoes them, and Ctrl+S saves the script. A bullet in the window title means there are unsaved edits. You'll be asked to save before any run.

Units are anchored to the video. Keep each unit's words in that unit; don't move a phrase into the next one.

Approve & Continue

Saves the script and runs the remaining stages: voice, synthesis and quality check. Units you edited by hand are kept even if you run Clean script again.
