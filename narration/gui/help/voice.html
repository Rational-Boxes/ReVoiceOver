Voice

ReVoiceOver speaks the cleaned script in a clone of your own voice. The clone is made from a short reference clip of your recording.

The reference clip

The Voice stage looks for the best 8–15 second stretch of continuous speech: no fillers, no long pauses, a confident transcript and a healthy level without distortion. It cleans it lightly (noise reduction, loudness) and saves it as voice_ref.wav in the work folder.

On the Voice tab you can:

  • Play Reference Clip to hear what the clone is based on.
  • Pick one of the other clean stretches: play it, then Use This One.
  • Use My Own Clip… to supply a separate recording (10–15 s of natural speech in a quiet room works best).
  • Pick Automatically to go back to the automatic choice.

Changing the reference re-runs the Voice stage. The synthesized clips then become stale and are re-made on the next run.

Registered voices

The clip is uploaded to DeepInfra once, which returns a voice id. ReVoiceOver remembers it in ~/.config/narr/voices.json, keyed by the clip's content, so the same clip is never uploaded twice, even for another video.

Privacy: registering a voice uploads the reference clip to DeepInfra and stores it with your account.

Voice models

Synthesis uses Zyphra/Zonos-v0.1-hybrid, which supports cloned voices, speaking rate and repeatable takes (seeds). Models without voice cloning cannot be used for synthesis.
