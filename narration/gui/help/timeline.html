Timeline

The timeline at the bottom shows the recording's waveform and, beneath it, one block per narration unit.

Reading it

  • A solid block is the spoken part of a unit; the thinner tail after it is the rest of its slot (the pause before the next unit).
  • A dashed outline marks a dropped unit: nothing will be spoken there.
  • A red outline needs attention (dropped content or a failed quality check); an amber one is worth a look.
  • The selected unit is highlighted, and the red line is the playhead.

Navigating

Mouse wheelScroll through time
Ctrl + wheel, + / -Zoom in and out at the pointer
DragPan

Home / EndJump to the start or end

Left / Right arrowPrevious / next unit

Playback

Double-click a unit, press Space, or use the play button to hear the original recording of the selected unit. Play the synthesized voice from the Script or Quality tab; the playhead then follows the new clip from the unit's start.
